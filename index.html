01 - PRODUCT THESIS

The internet already exists.
Its coordination layer does not.

Aura turns compatible routers, venues, and managed hotspots into a programmable connectivity network. The protocol rewards useful service, publishes verifiable settlement state, and gives builders a clean infrastructure surface.

02 - PROTOCOL LAYERS

Built like infrastructure,
not a campaign page.

Each layer has a narrow responsibility: physical connectivity, gateway verification, service accounting, reward logic, and on-chain settlement.

03 - NON-NEGOTIABLE BOUNDARY

Packets off-chain.
Coordination on-chain.

Aura does not route browsing traffic through Solana. Normal internet traffic remains normal IP traffic; Solana coordinates identity, ownership, staking, rewards, claims, treasury, and protocol state.
